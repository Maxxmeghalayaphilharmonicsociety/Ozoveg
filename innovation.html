What’s new

Why OZOVEG is different

Most post-harvest solutions do one thing — wash, or preserve. OZOVEG integrates organic washing with an edible coating system to address pesticide residues, moisture loss and premature spoilage together.

Two-in-one integration

Residue reduction and freshness preservation are coordinated in a single line, rather than bolted together from separate products.

Order by design

Clean first, then seal: the coating is only ever applied over a surface that has already been washed and rinsed.

Food-grade & edible

A four-component coating made from food-grade ingredients that the consumer can eat without noticing — no synthetic preservatives.

No persistent residue

The oxidative wash leaves nothing behind: ozone decays back to oxygen, and a potable rinse removes by-products.

Drop-in adoption

Six steps map onto existing receiving, washing, rinsing, dipping, drying and packing stations — an upgrade, not a rebuild.

Our approach

Scalable, science-driven solutions

Nutra Fresh LLP’s vision is to deliver scalable, science-driven solutions that benefit farmers, processors, retailers and consumers while promoting more efficient and sustainable food supply chains.

By combining residue reduction and freshness preservation in a coordinated process, we aim to support safer food handling, minimise post-harvest losses and improve the marketability of fresh produce.

  • Grounded in established water-treatment and food-coating science
  • Engineered for commercial throughput, not just the lab bench
  • Performance validated under defined processing conditions
